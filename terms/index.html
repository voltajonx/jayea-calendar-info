Terms of use

Prepared October 23, 2026 · Personal-use integration

These are the operating boundaries of JayEA Calendar, a personal-use integration controlled by its account owner. This website does not offer a public service, paid subscription, account registration or Google sign-in.

Owner-directed calendar assistance

Calendar changes require a specific, direct request from the authorized owner in the existing private assistant conversation. A request to review, suggest or draft is not permission to change the calendar. Missing or ambiguous timing and scheduling conflicts require clarification.

Supported actions and limits

The integration reads the authorized account’s primary calendar and creates or edits standalone personal events without guests. The assistant’s tool does not offer deletion, invitations, invitation responses, recurring-series edits, calendar-sharing changes or automatic rescheduling. Some events must be edited directly in Google Calendar.

Review important details

AI responses and external services can be wrong, delayed or unavailable. The owner should check important dates, timezones, locations and saved events in Google Calendar. A calendar entry does not book an appointment, reserve a service, invite another person or create a separate message reminder.

Access and availability

The owner may disconnect or stop using the integration. Availability depends on Google, the assistant service, its hosting and messaging providers. Moving Google authorization out of Testing removes that mode’s seven-day limit; it does not guarantee permanent access or eliminate every possible future sign-in.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains calendar-data access, processing, storage and disconnection. Do not provide passwords, authorization codes, patient information or other sensitive details in requests. Support is handled through the existing private conversation with JayEA or the account owner’s private assistant setup.
